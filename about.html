Who We Are

reka.ai is a Malaysia-based AI agent studio founded by Peter Chan Wei Soon, Nor Aniq Nor Azim, and Nur Loqman Hakim Mohd Nasir with practical engineering execution across fintech platforms, high-volume payment processing, and community technology initiatives.

We reject generic conversational chatbots. Instead, we architect autonomous, narrow-scope AI agents that integrate directly into your daily operational tools (WhatsApp, Excel, Google Sheets, CRMs, and internal ERPs) to systematically eliminate repetitive administrative overhead.
